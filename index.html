Our Story

Redefining Premium Mobility in India

Velora Drive was born from a simple observation: India's car rental experience was broken. Dirty cars, hidden charges, unprofessional service—the list went on.

We set out to build something different. A premium mobility service that treats every customer like a VIP, every vehicle like a masterpiece, and every journey like an experience to remember.

Founded in 2022, we've grown from a small fleet in Delhi to serving customers across five major Indian cities. Our commitment to quality, transparency, and customer satisfaction has earned us the trust of over 50,000 customers.
